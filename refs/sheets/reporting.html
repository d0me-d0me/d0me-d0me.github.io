Reporting

The deliverable is the report, not the shell. A field checklist for turning findings into something a reader can act on — structure, severity scoring, the anatomy of a single finding, evidence handling, and remediation language.成果物は shell ではなくレポートである。指摘を「読み手が行動できる形」に変えるための実務チェックリスト — 構成・深刻度・指摘の書き方・証跡・改善提案までを扱う。

01Report anatomy構成

A report serves two readers at once: the decision-maker who funds the fix and the engineer who applies it. Structure it so each finds their layer without reading the other's.

SectionAudienceAnswers
Executive summaryLeadershipWhat is the business risk, in one page, no jargon
Scope & rulesAllWhat was tested, what was off-limits, when
MethodologyTechnical / assuranceHow coverage was achieved; what a gap means
FindingsEngineersEach issue, ranked, with proof and fix
Remediation planOwnersWhat to do first, and by when
AppendixReferenceRaw output, tool versions, full host/port lists
rule of thumb The executive summary is written last and read first. If a reader stops after page one, they should still know the risk and the top three actions.
要点 / key takeaways
  • Two readers, two layers: business risk on top, reproducible detail below.
  • Scope and rules-of-engagement are part of the finding's meaning — an untested area is not a clean bill.

02Severity & scoring深刻度

Score consistently so findings rank against each other, not against mood. CVSS gives a defensible baseline; risk is that base adjusted for this environment's likelihood and business impact.

BandCVSS v3.1Typical meaning
Critical9.0–10.0Unauth remote to full compromise; fix now
High7.0–8.9Significant exposure, limited pre-conditions
Medium4.0–6.9Requires chaining or local access
Low0.1–3.9Hardening / defense-in-depth
Info0.0Observation, no direct risk

Record the full vector, not just the number — it shows the reasoning and lets the reader re-score for their context.

text
# CVSS v3.1 base vector (example: unauth RCE over network)
CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:U/C:H/I:H/A:H  # => 9.8 Critical

# CVSS v4.0 adds Attack Requirements (AT) and split user interaction
CVSS:4.0/AV:N/AC:L/AT:N/PR:N/UI:N/VC:H/VI:H/VA:H/SC:N/SI:N/SA:N
watch A CVSS base score is environment-agnostic. A "High" on an internet-facing auth endpoint and the same "High" on an isolated lab host are not the same risk — say so in the finding, do not let the number stand alone.
要点 / key takeaways
  • Risk = base severity adjusted for exploitability and business impact in this environment.
  • Always publish the vector string; a bare number is not reviewable.

03Anatomy of a finding指摘の書き方

One issue per finding. Each should stand alone: a reader jumping straight to it must understand what, why it matters, how it was proven, and what to do.

markdown
## <Concise, neutral title — what, where>
# Severity  <Critical|High|Medium|Low|Info>  (CVSS:3.1/AV:.../ => <score>)
# Affected  <host / endpoint / component / version>

### Description
<What the weakness is, in plain terms.>

### Impact
<What an attacker gains; tie to a business consequence.>

### Evidence
<Redacted request/response or screenshot ref; enough to believe it,
 not a weaponised recipe.>

### Reproduction
<Numbered steps at a level a defender can verify and re-test.>

### Remediation
<Specific, prioritised fix + a durable control.>

### References
<CWE / vendor advisory / standard.>
titles Neutral and specific beats dramatic. "Session cookie missing HttpOnly on /app" ages better and searches better than "Critical cookie flaw!".
要点 / key takeaways
  • One issue per finding; each is self-contained.
  • Evidence proves the issue without shipping an operational exploit.
  • Reproduction is written for the person who has to re-test the fix.

04Evidence & handling証跡

Evidence has to be believable and safe. It substantiates the finding while carrying no more sensitive data than proof requires.

handling / countermeasures
  • Redact secrets, tokens, PII and full credentials — show enough to prove, mask the rest.
  • Timestamp captures and note the source host; keep a private, access-controlled master set.
  • Prefer scoped proof (one record, one account) over mass extraction.
  • Store the report and its evidence encrypted; agree a retention and deletion window with the client.
watch A report is itself sensitive: it is a map of how to break in. Treat drafts, screenshots and appendices as client-confidential from the first capture, not just at delivery.
要点 / key takeaways
  • Prove, don't hoard: minimal evidence, redacted.
  • The deliverable and its working files are confidential end to end.

05Remediation language改善提案

The fix section is where a report earns its cost. Make it actionable, prioritised, and owned.

Instead ofWrite
"Improve security""Set SameSite=Lax and HttpOnly on the session cookie in /app"
"Patch the server""Upgrade component X to ≥ v1.2.4; the fix addresses CVE-YYYY-NNNNN"
"Use strong passwords""Enforce a 14-char minimum + breached-password check at the IdP"

Separate the immediate fix from the durable control: patch this host now (tactical), and add the missing detection or process so it does not recur (strategic).

要点 / key takeaways
  • Specific, verifiable actions — a reader should know exactly what to change.
  • Prioritise, and name an owner where possible.
  • Pair the point fix with a control that prevents the class of issue.
